# Fidelity tracker

Updated 2026-10-11 for version 0.14.0. This is a working comparison, not a claim of a verified 1:1 recreation. No Steam installation, original source, or extracted asset bundle is present in the workspace. The user chose the current Steam release as the parity target. Its installed build/version and exact formulas have not been measured. All sprites, scenery, names, sound effects, and balance shipped here are newly authored.

Destroyed planks, short-gap crossing, falls, restoration and safe damaged save recovery are delivered. Timed boarding, swimming and crew rescue are now delivered. A starter owned item/drop system and bounded eighteen-node seeded region are delivered. Full catalogues, world discovery/regions, terrain and original content coverage can now expand without bypassing navigation, persistence, or resource-lifetime tests. Measured evidence belongs in [implementation status](implementation-status.md).

Version 0.8.1 adds visible foreground grips and dedicated pistol aim/recoil arms, preserving independently equipped items and the existing cooldown-driven presentation. It still lacks continuous attack animation, reload, hurt and death frames.

Version 0.9.0 adds timed rope crossing and overboard recovery with Swimming and crew rescue lines. These are bounded authored mechanics; original physics, skill ranks and numerical balance remain unverified.

Version 0.9.1 adds original modular cabin/deck architecture and a pixel hull/sail/prop redraw based on the observed customization screenshot. Full original construction parts and furnishings remain a gap.

Version 0.10.0 adds a first owned construction inventory, decorative cabin fittings, port buy/sell, validated placements, once-only salvage and independent capture transfer. General equipment/consumable drops, full furnishings and interactive effects remain gaps.

Version 0.10.1 adds authored seeded sloop/brig hulls and two-masted boss flagships, real connected lower cabins, extra owned fittings and preserved capture geometry. This addresses repeating enemy silhouettes; it does not implement procedural world topology, original ship blueprints or structural physics. [Fleet evidence and limits](design/ship-fleet.md).

Version 0.10.2 enlarges authored ship interiors and adds horizontal camera focus without reducing crew size. Bidirectional body/item/finger layers follow movement and targets; vertical travel preserves direction. These are measured presentation/handling improvements, not claims of original-game proportions. [Review](design/roomier-ships.md).

Version 0.11.0 adds four targeted consumables, shared recovered weapons, immutable schema-7 migration, atomic trades/use, exactly-once seeded loot and a reproducible eighteen-node chart. Island widths vary while preserving reachable spawns/chests. These close starter inventory/generation gaps; values, topology and names remain authored. [Contract, inspected captures and coverage limits](design/loot-and-seeded-world.md).

Version 0.12.0 carries melee, ranged and an accessory independently, retaining personal lockers and recovered ownership. Combat chooses a blade up close or pistol farther away using the existing shared cooldown; intact hull cells block shots. Two first accessories add deck/ladder speed or suppress wearer dirt. Values/AI are authored, and independent reload timers, full gear catalogue and exact current-Steam rules remain gaps. [Contract and evidence](design/crew-loadouts.md).

Version 0.13.0 adds six authored attribute effects, earned points and persistent groups. [Reference distinctions and actual behavior](design/crew-progression.md). The user-selected current Steam release remains the target; exact installed formulas, skill ranks, point rates and group count have not been measured.

Version 0.14.0 adds owned books, matching-book ranks and work XP, supported in category by the developer’s 2023 Steam patch notes and dated gameplay coverage. All numeric effects/caps/costs are expressly authored. Visible living bodies receive canvas hits at their actual rendered position and display order. [Contract and primary reference](design/skill-books.md). Exact current Steam balance, full skills and content remain unmeasured.
